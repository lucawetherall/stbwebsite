**23rd August 2026**

### Your weekly St Barnabas Ealing newsletter

Greetings to you all,

We have enjoyed a cooling of temperatures this week and it has been good to see people out and about in the Lane. For those of us who are unwell and at home, you have been uppermost in our thoughts and indeed our prayers.  Some are on the public prayer list and some prefer to remain known only to Ministry Team.  God knows your needs and is present whatever we seek to express in parish - I hope you sense this to be so.

This week we have launched our search for our new Office Manager, and already have received some expressions of interest.  Ideally, this might be a local person, so if you or someone you know might fit this role, please do pass the job details on. The hours fit inside school drop off - or might be ideal for a recent graduate looking to combine this employment/pension with another career initiative. Details can be accessed via our homepage of [our website](https://www.barnabites.com/) or directly [on this link](https://api2.churchdesk.com/files/03631d98-ba32-4c39-a3f1-876510c11829/view).

Please keep this recruitment process in your prayers - prayer is surprisingly effective!!

Prayers and best wishes until I next see you,

**Mother Sarah**

On about 16 September we say a fond farewell to Sanjit Sil, our Office Manager of the last 3 and a half years. Sanjit is a highly sought-after teacher of English for Speakers of Other Languages (ESOL), and has worked at St Barnabas alongside his career in that field. He has been invited to expand his work with Ealing Council and West London College - we wish him very well in this enhanced role.

Sanjit arrived in post here just as Fr Justin was leaving, and held the fort over the vacancy in a way which speaks volumes about his values of service and loyalty. He is seen here at the Summer Fayre - again getting involved. We will miss him.

You can [donate for Sanjit's leaving gift here](https://donate.churchdesk.com/en-gb/1/LQ5r2qQDTn) and a card will be in church for messages.
