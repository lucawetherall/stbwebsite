Eastertide continues, and we are called to embrace this new life in all its challenges and joys, looking always for signs of the risen Christ in our midst, no matter how unexpected. Last week's message highlighted such important parts of our church community that I think it bears a second reading (or so I say!):

As we approach our APCM (annual parish meeting), we can be joyful at the signs of energy and faithful love of this place and what it can be.  We have no fewer than 4 new nominated members of the PCC, one of whom is to be our new Treasurer and another to be churchwarden.  Also, two PCC members have extended their service by being deanery synod representatives in this important year for the CofE, alongside our third new rep. 

This is no ordinary annual meeting - it is (to borrow a phrase) a St Barnabas Ealing annual meeting.

Take a look below at the report which highlights all the parts of our church life - including many parts of it that you may not yet know anything about! You can ask to be put in touch with those who lead bits that interest you and that you might like to know more about or be part of - just let me know or email the office. Our finances are hugely improved on last year, but we still need to ask for your support to make all these parts of our collective life happen - PLEASE GIVE AS GENEROUSLY AS YOU CAN (the info is below).

This Sunday we will join in shared music and voice as we welcome Luca Wetherall to our community and shared life. [You can read more about Luca on our website.](https://www.barnabites.org/about-us/whos-who) Do stay on after Mass - or join from Noisy Mass - and have a chance to say hi.

With love and prayers for a continued Eastertide, 

**Mother Sarah**

I am reading: _Enlightenment_, by Sarah Perry
