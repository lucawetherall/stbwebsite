Ealing Flower Festival - Cinema in Bloom

Get ready for a blooming good time at this year's three‑day Flower Festival celebrating the 110th anniversary of St Barnabas Church and Ealing's rich cinema history.

Enjoy beautiful themed floral displays, refreshments, demonstrations and hands‑on workshops.

Our theme, Cinema in Bloom, brings your favourite films to life through floral art. Come wander, explore, and celebrate with us among the flowers!

Thu 16 - Sat 18 July

Hours Thursday and Friday: Noon to 6pm Saturday: 10am to 4pm. Last admissions: 30 minutes before closing time
