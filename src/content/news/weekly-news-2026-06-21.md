Many thanks to everyone who came to our Summer Fayre last Sunday.  To the many  who set up behind the scenes on Saturday and Sunday, and packed away and counted money; to those who baked cakes and sorted toys and set up games and crafts; to the food team and the bar team; and to our fabulous Band and to Michael who collectively kept the music going throughout.

Our Summer Fayre was a great success - and even blessed with fair weather!  Overall, we also raised around £2000 for church funds.

It was a pleasure to welcome the Brentham Society once again as we build stronger links.

We enjoyed before it a wonderful Patronal Mass, with glorious flowers thanks to the Team, and a sermon full of a sense of place and our calling from Mthr Jeanny, and intercessions from youth group and profound musical offerings.

We take things a little slower this week!

with prayers and best wishes,

**Mother Sarah**
