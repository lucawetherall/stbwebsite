This Sunday we welcome ten young people and their families and friends as they are admitted to Holy Communion, and also the family of William as he is baptised. It is also the Sunday on which we hold our Annual Parochial Church Meeting.

It can be tempting to see these as two separate types of things: one about families and relationships and spirituality and the other about administration, rules and committees.

It so happens I love both - and that may be because to me they are different versions of the same thing: signs of energies, of love and of the workings of God. I am fond of the phrase "holy admin" and taunt my fellow clergy with it, perhaps very naughtily, when they are rolling their eyes about meetings, diaries and emails.

The same care that goes into making the commitment to venture into receiving the sacrament of the eucharist is the care that shows up, week after week to make sure things are still on the road and others are supported.

In all of this I feel we are shaped - formed, if you like - as followers of Christ and as people. The energies of the Holy Spirit are particularly vibrant at Ascensiontide and Pentecost, which makes it a fine time to be doing both these sets of thing.  Let's see how they combine in the Mass, APCM and Choral Evensong that Sunday will usher in!

**Mother Sarah**

I am reading: _Lonely Planet Sri Lanka; Theo of Golden,_ Allen Levi

Candle wax recycling

Richard from [recycledwax.co.uk](https://recycledwax.co.uk/) came on Sunday to collect our old paschal candles!
