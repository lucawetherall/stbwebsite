Mid-August greetings

It has been a refreshing break for me over the past two weeks and I have enjoyed the time away with family and the change of pace in Amsterdam and in South Devon!

It has been lovely start catching up with some of you as gentle work and preparations keep rolling over the summer break.

This Sunday I look forward to being in Mass with those of you who are around and able. Many thanks to Mthr Valerie for presiding last week, and during the early part of this week with Felicity.

Prayers and best wishes until I next see you,

**Mother Sarah**

This week I am reading chapters of: After Whiteness - Willie James Jennings

At the beginning of September we say a fond farewell to Sanjit Sil, our Office Manager of the last 3 and a half years. Sanjit is a highly sought-after teacher of English for Speakers of Other Languages (ESOL), and has worked at St Barnabas alongside his career in that field. He has been invited to expand his work with Ealing Council and West London College - we wish him very well in this enhanced role.

Sanjit arrived in post here just as Fr Justin was leaving, and held the fort over the vacancy in a way which speaks volumes about his values of service and loyalty. He is seen here at the Summer Fayre - again getting involved. We will miss him.

We will organise a way for members of St Barnabas community to offer him our best wishes - details in due course.

Please speak to Mthr Sarah, John Newbegin or Richard Jones (Treasurer) if you'd like to know more about the vacancy Sanjit leaves.
