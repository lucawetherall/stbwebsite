Our Summer Fayre is on Sunday 14 June at 12noon-4pm!

**This year we start early - so food and drink are available on the spot - don't go home after church - feed your children and yourselves at our BBQ!**

Live music (our Jazz Band with our new Director of Music on bass - and a set by Michael Hill from Noisy Mass and Instagram!)

Children's competitions, crafts, toy sale, and much more - run by Chatty Church.

Drinks served to your table on the lawn all afternoon.

Relax, chat, try the tombola, take cake slices away for the week!

**Our Patronal Sung Mass is at 10.30 - if you have one, do wear a red rose in your lapel as we give thanks for the encouragement of Barnabas**. With thanks to our flowers team who have decked the church with red blooms.

I look forward to seeing those of you who can there - and we hold our whole newsletter family in prayer on this feast of encouragement and connection.  Prayers and God's blessing to all,

**Mother Sarah**
