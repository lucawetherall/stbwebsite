As the month of May wends its way, and Pitshanger folk turn their thoughts to May Day and - at St Barnabas - the coming feasts of Ascension and Pentecost, there is a definite smell of spring in the air.

Eastertide proves to us that it is as long as Lent (longer, indeed) and the Gospel of John weaves the poetry and teaching of Jesus with the relationship we are all called to in the name of the Father, Son and Holy Spirit. As our Lady Chapel reredos proclaims, "I am with you always" - so also Jesus assures us in the gospel this Sunday "I will ask the Father, and he will give you another Advocate, to be with you for ever. This is the Spirit of truth".  It is all relationship.

The world seeks all over, yet what it yearns for is there all along.

May your Eastertide blessings continue, with grace and truth,

**Mother Sarah**

I am reading: _Enlightenment_, by Sarah Perry
