Warmest greetings for Lent,

We look forward as every year to greeting Palm Sunday with our procession for the Liturgy of the Palms and then the Mass in church.  Do note that this Sunday **the clocks change** and we lose an hour!  Come to church and collect a service sheet and hymn book before making your way to the Village Inn.  If you prefer to stay in church, the Liturgy of the Palms will be observed there too, led by Mthr Valerie.

A couple of other notices:

Harry ends his time as Director of Music on Easter Day.  This Sunday (Palm Sunday) there will be **drinks after the service for Harry**.   Everyone welcome to come and thank him and wish him well.  The link for contributions to his leaving gift is below.

Good Friday Walk of Witness - come and join us on the Walk, starting St Barnabas at 9.30.  PLEASE if you are walking, would you consider being **a steward**?  Please let me or John Newbegin know if you can - it is a straightforward job, clearly explained on the day.

With love and prayers for a blessed Passiontide,

**Mother Sarah**

Harry Guthrie ends his role as Director of Music on 5 April 2026. He joined us on Ascension Day 2023, and over the past three years, he has revolutionised our choir and brought joy back into singing choral music. Regular Sunday evening Choral Evensongs feature on the calendar, and we now sing psalms set to Anglican Chant each Sunday morning. We have sung a wide range of mass settings by Haydn, Mozart, Duruflé and Darke, as well as a very broad range of anthems, both ancient & modern. A particular highlight has been the introduction of some contemporary female composers' works. Harry led a very successful 'Come & Sing' event in 2025 and last month the choir took part in a superb joint concert with Ealing Junior Music School Adult Chorale. Above all, we have been a happy band of choristers under his leadership, and it has been immensely enjoyable. He will be missed enormously. His last service will be the Easter Sunday Mass at 10.30am on 5 April 2026.
