Electoral Roll

Everyone is able to join the electoral roll if they worship here or if they live in the parish boundary.

[You can join anytime, by completing this form](https://api2.churchdesk.com/files/61548745-5a8d-40b9-99d3-bbe3981f84dd/view) and dropping it into the Parish Office letterbox.

Being on the electoral roll can make a difference with school applications, as well as being a part of our belonging and commitment within our Christian faith.
