Preparations continue at real pace for our Summer Fayre - this year on Sunday 14 June at 12noon-4pm.

**This year we start early - so food and drink are available on the spot - don't go home after church - feed your children and yourselves at our BBQ!**

Please spread the word - check out our Facebook posts and share them - and most of all please come and bring your family and friends.

This is truly a case of the more the merrier - and also is one of our key fundraiser events for the year.  Please contribute to the life and work of St Barnabas by coming and enjoying food and drink and lots of stalls and activities for all ages.

Our live music goes around the clock, featuring Instagram sensation and local legend, singer-songwriter @michaelhill\_music. Hear also our jazz band, feat. our new Director of Music as freshest collaborator.

Drinks served to your table on the lawn all afternoon.

Relax, chat, try the tombola, take cake slices away for the week!

**Mother Sarah**

I am reading: _Theo of Golden,_ Allen Levi
