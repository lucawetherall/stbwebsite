Alongside the Mass on Sundays and through the week, the year is marked by the great feasts of the
Church, by music, and by the ordinary hospitality of a parish — the Food Pantry, the Memory Café,
Pitshanger Pictures and much besides. Everyone is welcome at all of it, whether you belong here
already or are simply curious.

Dates are also announced in our [Weekly News](/news) and on the noticeboards along the Lane.
