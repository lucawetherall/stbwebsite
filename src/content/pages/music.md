We are fortunate to have a well-established and thriving choir, rooted in the Anglican choral
tradition, that leads our musical offering and helps us encounter God in our common worship. The
choir sings the **Sung Mass every Sunday at 10.30am** — a sung setting, hymns, a sung psalm and a
communion anthem — and also sings the great festivals of the year. **Choral Evensong** is sung on
the first Sunday of the month, save for months when a sung festival service takes its place in the
evening.

Our choir is led by our Director of Music (Luca) and our Organist (Hugh), with paid choral
scholars and a body of voluntary singers. [Meet our musicians](/music/our-musicians).

Many of our services are livestreamed and archived on our
[YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@stbarnabasealing2270), where you can hear the choir and
the organ. You are always welcome to come and listen in person.
