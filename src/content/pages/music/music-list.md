Each service below shows the setting, psalm and anthems the choir will sing. The list runs to the
end of the choir year and is kept up to date as the terms are planned; the next service is marked
at the top. This page prints tidily if you would like a copy for the choir stalls.

Visitors are always welcome to come and listen, and singers are always welcome to join us —
[meet our musicians](/music/our-musicians) to find out more.
