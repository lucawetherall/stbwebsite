The list opens on the month ahead, with the next service marked. Step through the year with the
arrows on each month, or choose a month from the bar. Each service shows the setting, psalm and
anthems the choir will sing; the page prints as a full booklet if you would like a copy for the
choir stalls.

Visitors are always welcome to come and listen, and singers are always welcome to join us —
[meet our musicians](/music/our-musicians) to find out more.
